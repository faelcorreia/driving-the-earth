world.bin: world map for the home picker (F17b). Generated by scripts/build_worldmap.py.

Source data: Natural Earth 1:50m physical and cultural vectors (land, admin-0 boundary lines)
and 1:10m populated places (simple), read from the GeoJSON files of nvkelso/natural-earth-vector at commit
ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19 (sha256 checked by the script).
Streets and neighborhoods are not in this file: they come on demand as OpenFreeMap vector tiles
(https://openfreemap.org, no key), which are built from OpenStreetMap data (ODbL, "© OpenStreetMap
contributors"). Attributed in the picker: "© OpenStreetMap contributors, OpenFreeMap".
Natural Earth data is in the public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
